# Code of Conduct - Winterhold Core

## Our Pledge
In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to making participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards of Behavior
We adhere to the **Contributor Covenant v2.1** standard. Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:
* Using welcoming and inclusive language.
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
* Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
* Focusing on what is best for the community.

## Ethical Framework & Technological Responsibility
As a project centered on Sovereign AI and Inclusive Robotics, we enforce strict ethical mandates:
1. **Inclusion Priority:** Contributions must not introduce discriminatory biases against individuals with disabilities.
2. **Data Sovereignty:** The codebase must adhere to *Privacy by Design* principles, ensuring user autonomy over their data.
3. **Peaceful Purpose:** This project explicitly prohibits the use of its infrastructure for the development of autonomous weaponry or mass surveillance systems.
